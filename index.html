Yiwen Fang

Postdoctoral Researcher, ZJU–UIUC Institute
Ph.D. Civil Engineering, UCLA, 2023

I estimate how much water is stored in mountain snow — at resolutions no satellite measures directly — and build the physics-grounded AI systems that make that kind of modelling reproducible for other researchers.

Seasonal snowpack is the primary freshwater reservoir for the Western United States, High Mountain Asia and northwest China, and among the least-constrained terms in the water balance. Ground networks sit low, flat and accessible; the snow does not. Passive microwave retrievals saturate where the snow is deepest. So the amount has to be inferred rather than measured — from what melts, and when.

My doctoral work at UCLA with Steven A. Margulis produced two Bayesian snow reanalyses now archived at NASA NSIDC and used as benchmark records by water managers and machine-learning researchers. At ZJUI I lead RSHub, a platform that puts physics-based microwave scattering models behind a conversation.
