Software and platforms

RSHub — Remote Sensing Hub

A cloud platform unifying our group's soil, vegetation and snow microwave scattering models behind one interface, with natural-language access to physics-based radiative transfer runs. It lets a researcher explore how land-surface variables manifest across frequency bands — directly informing sensor selection, mission design and retrieval algorithm development.

I directed a team of four undergraduate and four graduate students through the full cycle: architecture, model integration, interface, deployment. An autonomous-agent successor, RSHub 2.0, is under review.

Snow storage uncertainty — midlatitude American Cordillera

An intercomparison of global and regional snow products across two mountain systems, against 480-metre and 100-metre reanalysis references.

Products coarser than 10 km underestimate peak storage substantially. Only sub-kilometre products reproduce the windward–leeward pattern that orography imposes. Precipitation forcing explains most of the snowfall variance; model process differences explain much of the rest.

Altay snow data assimilation and InSAR

Multi-year 150-metre snow water equivalent over Altay, Xinjiang, from assimilated Harmonized Landsat–Sentinel fractional snow-covered area, validated against 2025 snow-season field campaigns. Ongoing work evaluates multispectral unmixing and differential interferometry as assimilation constraints.
